Every day at the academy is structured to balance training, education, and personal development. Our players follow a routine that builds discipline, fitness, and football intelligence.

Beyond the Pitch

More Than Football

Academic Support

We support our players' education through homework help, study sessions, and partnerships with local schools to ensure academic success alongside sporting development.

Character Building

Through mentorship and life skills workshops, we help young players develop discipline, respect, leadership, and responsibility that serves them throughout life.

Community Service

Our players participate in community outreach programs, learning to give back and use their platform as athletes to make a positive impact in Limuru and beyond.

Family Days

Regular events where parents and families come together to watch matches, celebrate achievements, and connect with the academy community.

Award Ceremonies

Annual awards recognizing outstanding performance, sportsmanship, academic achievement, and most improved players across all age groups.

Tournament Travel

Players travel to compete in tournaments across Kenya, gaining valuable competitive experience and creating lasting memories with teammates.
